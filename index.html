A Multi-Instrumental Musician

I am a multi-instrumental musician, proficient in performing on various musical instruments, including classical guitar, piano, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, bass guitar, ukulele, and Ukrainian folk instruments.

With my music education, I have dedicated almost 20 years to teaching children how to play guitar and piano. My mastery of different musical styles—ranging from Baroque and classical to jazz, rock, and pop—enables me to connect with students from all cultural and musical traditions.
