Musical Achievements

A story of performance, collaboration and teaching, from Ukraine to Dubai.

2004–2014 · Ukraine

Performance Highlights

From concert halls in Ukraine to live performances in Dubai.

Between 2004 and 2014, Kateryna performed at the Donetsk and Dnipro Philharmonics and other cultural venues across Ukraine.

2015–2020 · Dubai

Performing in Dubai

Bringing live music to audiences across the city.

While working with Brooklyn Melodies Music Center, Kateryna took part in public performances at venues including Dubai Mall, City Walk and local schools.

2011–2014 · Ukraine

Workshops & Festivals

Sharing musical knowledge beyond the classroom.

Kateryna gave an open lecture on guitar teaching in 2014 and helped organize the Crystal Strings Guitar Festival in Donetsk from 2011 to 2013.

2015–2020 · Dubai

Musical Projects

A collaborative approach to music education.

She regularly participated in projects and performances organized by Brooklyn Melodies Music Center.

Since 2023 · Dubai

Student Achievements

Celebrating the progress of the next generation.

Kateryna has organized concerts for her students in Dubai since 2023. Over her teaching career, students have continued their musical education and received recognition in music competitions.
